## 2. The indictments, each with frames and its mechanism

### 2.1 One selection wore FOUR layers of chrome (t31–38, again t70–76, worst t97–107)

Full-res t33.5, all simultaneously visible for one pen selection of はまもなく:

1. the v1.3.0 echo menu (head 「はまもなく」, rows ⚡分類して台帳へ / 🔍辞書で引く);
2. the entry's own standing Copy | Insert | 💾Save | 🏷️分類 panel;
3. the dock bar carrying the phrase with 語彙 | 辞書 | X検索 destinations;
4. iOS's Copy / Writing Tools bar, arriving ~1.5s late, ON TOP.

This is IMG_1197's 「three menus on one selection」 defect *rebuilt with one
more menu* — the v1.3.0 round added the named-verb menu without retiring
what it superseded. The ガッツリ round (t97–107) is ~10 unbroken seconds of
menu wrestling: the native bar returns three times, expands to its full
Copy|Writing Tools|Look Up|Translate form, and the kana keyboard pops in
uninvited twice (t111.5, t113.3). **The felt "really not good" lives here.**

→ REPAIRED (this round): the standing panel is gone (2.3); the echo waits
480ms on slate touch/pen so the native bar lands first and the echo takes
BELOW (selection-echo.ts, the churn fix); chrome selections no longer arm
the echo (2.5). The dock bar is legitimate chrome (it is the search box +
suite nav, not a selection answer) and stays. The native bar itself is the
webview floor: a web plugin cannot merge verbs into the system menu. Two
pieces of chrome on one selection is the honest minimum; four was not.

### 2.2 A selection NAVIGATED the page away (t26.8–t31.3)

Full-res t31.3: breadcrumb 「◀戻る ガッツリ → 彼らの飛行機はまもなく」, page body
「"彼らの飛行機はまもなく" — no results」. The user selected inside the entry's
example line; the base page threw ガッツリ away and searched the whole
sentence, landing on a no-results page — and the sentence entered BOTH the
walk trail and the permanent dated History.

Mechanism, read in code: `makeJapaneseClickable` wraps **maximal Japanese
runs** — 「彼らの飛行機はまもなく」 is ONE span, because the headword mark splits
the text node around 飛び立つ. A settled tap on that span handed the whole
run to the lookup/peek road. The unit of a tap was a RUN; Monokakido's unit
is the word under the finger.

→ REPAIRED: the tap now resolves through `wordAtPoint` (the one shared
resolver: longest dictionary-confirmed string from the tapped character,
probes ≤8 chars, deinflection included) and peeks THAT. Dictionary-or-
nothing: a short unknown run still peeks (honest 該当なし); a long one does
nothing — Monokakido's own tap behaviour. And the History records only
pages that ANSWERED (all three record sites gated; the sidecar's late
answer records where it lands). A miss is not a place.

### 2.3 The entry wore a standing control panel (t139, no selection active)

lower half of the article is Copy | Insert | 💾Save | 🏷️分類 + 🔗Context,

The Monokakido contrast is exact: its articles carry NO standing verbs.
Verbs arrive on demand and **name their object** — t168–173 「Add Idiom to
Bookmarks」, t183–185 「Add Example to Bookmarks」, t199–202 again — the

→ REPAIRED: the panel is deleted. The headword's verbs fold behind a quiet
⋯ on the header row, opening a vertical menu whose every row carries its
object: 「ガッツリ」をコピー ・ をノートに挿入 ・ を保存 ・ を台帳へ分類. One
glyph where there were four buttons; the row insert appears only when an
editor is actually there to receive it.

## 3. The Monokakido half — what the contrast actually teaches

- **One selection, one answer** (t169.75–172.25): the long menu rises,
  full-width readable rows, verbs naming scopes; Cancel and the reading
  surface is back instantly. No second surface ever appears.
- **The article is silent.** Entries carry text, cross-reference marks, and
  nothing else. All verbs are summoned.
- **縦書き selection is calm** (t214–225, t254–260): a tan band that grows
  by column; no navigation, no chrome, the selection is a *state of the
  text*, not an event that spawns UI.
- **Dictionary switching is a flip** (t226–230, t296–299): MEIKYO's list →
  entry → back, the same axis grammar the plugin's §30 build follows.
- **The keyboard appears exactly when summoned** (flick keyboard, t153+)
  and never otherwise — against the plugin half's two uninvited pop-ins.

**Anti-copy clause.** Not imported, on purpose: the native long-menu itself
(a webview cannot merge into iOS's menu — imitating its look while stacking
under the real one is the worst of both); Word/Idiom/Example as tabs (the
plugin's scope chips すべて/見出し/本文 already carry this per §30.6, and its
search grammar is space-AND, not field tabs); bookmark folders (the 台帳's
six classes are the filing grammar and colors are reserved); light theme.
