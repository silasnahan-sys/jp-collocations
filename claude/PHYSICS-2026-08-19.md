## §0 本質 — the thing itself

Every intuition on film, June through August, is one sentence:

> **The hand treats language as material — things to pick up, carry, cut, and
> rearrange while the world keeps moving — and the plugin treats language as
> strings to be transcribed into forms inside rooms.**

The interface being reached for is **the user's own notebook, made live**
(July 1: four capture kinds on one page, zero mode-switching; 〜 as an
ink-native grab-and-gap gesture; machine reconciles, hand ratifies).

Six verbs cover every gesture attempted in eight videos:

| 掴む grab | any Japanese on any surface becomes a held thing | 1082 t25 travel-to-分類; 1144 ink; まずは silence |
| 運ぶ carry | held while the world stays navigable beneath | 15s drag-ghost; five-app loop; tray dragged TO ink. Done right: Calendar (1159) |

| 並べ替える rearrange | the class experiment PERFORMED (並べ替えても生きる→🟠 / 死ぬ→◆) | the class definitions are tests nothing in the app can run |
| 見定める verdict | ✓/✕/skip on any machine offer, where it appears | the unpaid 48s triage stretch; 2 ratify rows ever |

Two world-laws: **世界は止まらない** (no rooms; capture parasitic on reading) ·
**戻りは無料** (return is the cheapest op, one fixed stroke from anywhere).
One machine-law: **機械は差し出す** (labors and offers at attention with a why;
never fills a hole).

**The physics runs in both directions.** Production (産出 — the missing wing)
is the same verbs pointed outward: grab from the 台帳, carry into a draft,
rearrange until the corpus adjudicates it natural (the book-club loop), with
願い as the front door — a production want IS a Reach.

## §2 器官 — the organs the other apps hold

The feel is a body of organs scattered across closed apps. The plugin — the
only owned surface, the only composition point — has the one organ none of
them have (the recall machine: corpus, sweep, 願い, 分類) and none of the
organs all of them have.

- **Monokakido — the place.** Locality, fixed stations, free return, sovereign
  selection. Stops at: no memory of you; entered only by a string you have.
  The user ENJOYS this one — follow the concept, then surpass it (Move 1½):
  keep paddles/ghost/full-replace/symmetric return; beat it with (a) history
  as a walkable trail layer (suite-nav goTo's truncate-back, unrendered),
  (b) selection that KEEPS (specimen chip = their selection + memory),
  (c) scene-carrying arrivals (the entry knows the sentence that sent you),
  (d) ▾ generalized to four authorities + entry by what you DON'T have.
  Benchmark footage: the 07-15 Monokakido ScreenRecording on Drive — the
  recording §26 was built from, never frame-gridded; pull via the release
  route and grid before the nav build. Acceptance: same walk filmed side by
  side, Monokakido vs plugin.
- **Apple Calendar — the carry and the slack (遊び).** Held object over a live
  world; toss without care; live-sync panel. Stops at: shallow objects, no
  interior to carve.
- **Apple Notes — the pen.** Scribble-grade zero-mode capture, ink
  smart-select. Stops at: the ink is terminal; no machine behind the page.
- **iPadOS — the anchoring.** OS verbs arrive attached to the thing touched;
  it wins every collision because it already behaves like the physics.
  Stops at: generic verbs, never yours.
- **X — the stream.** Attestation supply. Stops at: no net.
- **claude.ai — the adjudicator.** The production loop. Stops at: not at hand,
  session-bound.

One line: Monokakido gives place without memory; Calendar gives physics
without depth; Notes gives the pen without the machine; iOS gives anchoring
without your verbs; X gives the stream without a net — and the plugin has the
machine, offered through forms inside rooms. **The build is a transplant.**

**Membranes:** the OS will never admit the plugin into Notes or the manga
reader; the five-app loop is permanent. Law of a membrane (S-series): *what
you knew must arrive* — every crossing lands as a scene-carrying tray mark;
the machine reconciles on the far side. Two cheap membrane debts: the unrun
ink experiment (Pencil-drag selected ink into ここにドロップ — drop-router
already reads text/plain), and the founding trigger (squeeze/hotkey capture
at attention from any app — the July iOS denial the plugin never inherited).

## §3 何故 — why agents keep failing, and the inversion

Agents optimize for what they can verify without hands, so "done" migrates to
the pure core (12 checks, zero consumers) and feel-work gets deferred wearing
the user's own doctrine ("judged on glass") as a permission slip — when the
actual rule is *ship crude with the knob exposed*. Fix: **make feel checkable.
The camera is the CI** — every build ships a 90-second film script (numbered
actions, expected feelings); the user films and speaks one sentence per
wrongness. The film is the acceptance test, the regression baseline, and the
next spec. The loop (film → fix → re-film → confirmed) has NEVER closed once;
close it before building anything big, because feel is a memory and the hand
stays careful until the interface visibly catches a few tosses. Steering
metric: **the streak** — consecutive closed loops without dropped work.

## §5 問い — the recursive probe (言えるか), parsed and adjudicated

The 印象として brief performs nine moves: entry by DRAFT (thought, not typed
lookup) → situated fit → rearrangement (は↔がのは) → RE-ANCHORING (each answer
becomes the next frame — the load-bearing property; one-shot search cannot
answer even one chain) → paradigm at a slot → dispersal/interveners → register
fit → paragraph-scale association → "recursive stuff must be" (correct: the
tool is a DIALOGUE with the corpus). Those moves ARE the six verbs run against
the corpus — the specimen is the draft; capture complaints and query ambitions
are one architecture from two sides.

Deductive laws: ① compare only same-function alternatives (は vs がのは is a
presupposition difference — condition on discourse move; same law as
relevance-per-note-type and paradigm-is-frame-relative). ② at ~976K chars,
silence is the modal answer → the machine's first duty is attacking the
query's own over-specification; the descent ladder IS the generalized
deinflector (= X relevance's true-hit rung 0). ③ the intervener theorem: the
material between anchors is the identity — derived independently twice
(connector profile, Aug 12; 介在プロフィール, Aug 19) = strongest convergence
in the project; computable on the existing bigram index.

Adjudication of the fourth-pass design: KEEP silence-first, verify-first,
position-entropy pre-shading, closed operators + stop-rules, and the
cleft pushback. ITS DEEPEST MOVE, under-sold: a question = a capture with
attestations:[] — a wondering becomes a standing 台帳 entry and the EXISTING
sweep/願い machinery answers it over time. The probe is a REACH LADDER
(§27 given rungs); almost no new retrieval machinery is needed — the question
must be storable and the operators must be gestures. MISSED: operators have no
hand (front door = the draft/Reach, not a search box); no horizontal step
(silent rung → hold it and switch authority: TWC / 用例.jp / live X — the
descent ladder and Move 1½'s ▾ authority-cycle are one structure); and it was
the fourth unsaved doc (cured here). Danger: recursion vs immersion — any rung
must be droppable into the tray as a standing question; return free; the sweep
works it while you read. Consequence for the path: Move 3 += file wonderings
as empty-attestation entries; Move 4's compose buffer rides the ladder;
X relevance + probe + 産出 = ONE build from three angles.

   reversible work plus its film script. Reversibility is the safety.
2. **The camera is the CI.** Nothing is done until its film script ran on
   glass.
3. **Enter through the motivating example.** No certification until a hand
   performs the founding case end-to-end (こんな人間になる予定ではなかった's
   lattice; はず(。)〜まずは accruing a real sighting).

   against code; two audits found five false-believed states in one evening.
5. **Canon lives in git** — commit the analysis passes and this file; fix
   context.md's dead SHAs; merge canon with the work branch.
6. **The streak is the metric.**
