# 運びと遊び — Apple Calendar on film, three reels (2026-08-24)

Placed here untracked, same contract as PHYSICS-2026-08-19: delete to reject,
commit to adopt. Source footage: IMG_1212 (18s), IMG_1213 (30s), IMG_1214 (54s),
all frame-gridded at 2fps with 6fps closeups on the gesture windows. The three
reels are ONE eight-minute session — the Calendar now-line badge ticks 5:35 →
5:37 → 5:38 across them, which is also the first finding: the user filmed
themselves arranging ゆる勉強 blocks AROUND THE LITERAL PRESENT MOMENT, playing
the object against "now."

The prior canon gave Calendar one line: *"the carry and the slack (遊び). Held
object over a live world; toss without care; live-sync panel. Stops at: shallow
objects, no interior to carve."* These reels do not change the verdict — they
reveal that the physics under that line is much richer than "carry," and every
piece of it is buildable on surfaces we own. This is not a copying brief. It is
the grammar the hand already speaks, extracted so the plugin can speak it with
objects that HAVE interiors.

## §1 台帳 — what is actually on film

Aug 24, 5PM–6PM." Two truths co-visible the whole time: what it IS and what it

**IMG_1214 (minutes later; now-line 5:38).** t2.5–5.2: resize — the pencil

import. A hole, kept open.

## §2 法 — specific → general, each with its plugin consequence

1. **二重写し — one object, two projections.** Canvas block and editor panel
   are the same object seen twice; manipulating either leaves the other live;
   the editor is a mirror, not a room. *Filmed:* 1212 whole reel; 1213 header
   rewrite on release. *Consequence:* Move 2's inspector is exactly this and
   nothing less — TokenCanvas marks ON the card, modal demoted to a docked
   live panel. New sharpening from film: the panel must render **stored vs
   candidate as two registers** — during any drag/trim the panel keeps the
   stored value, the candidate rides the pointer, and the commit-on-release
   rewrites the panel visibly. No intermediate half-states written.

2. **宛名札 — the carried thing is always addressed.** While held, the
   candidate address rides the object (:15 chips); when the landing slot
   leaves the visible world, the FULL address pins to the edge being pushed
   ("8/25/26, 2:45PM"). The world never lets a held thing become "somewhere."
   *Consequence:* the hold-dock flick and every drop-router drag should carry
   an address chip — what this lands AS and WHERE (「→ トレイ ・ 🟠 ・
   scene乗車」), and when the destination surface is off-screen, an edge
   readout names it. This is S-law "what you knew must arrive" made visible
   DURING the throw instead of audited after it.

3. **鋳造 — duplicate is a one-gesture mint, and copies coexist.** Long-press
   at the object → menu at the object → the copy lands beside its sibling,
   lanes sharing the slot, ready to be thrown. Variant-making costs nothing
   and demands no aim. *Consequence:* specimen chips and tray cards get
   long-press → 複製 at the chip; duplicates land BESIDE the original (lane
   layout, no collision, no prompt). This is the missing substrate for Move
   3's 並べ替える — variants minted from a specimen and thrown into slots is
   the class experiment as a hand gesture.

4. **両利きの器官 — effectors are interchangeable mid-flow.** Finger presses
   while the pencil stays cradled; pencil takes over one second later; no
   mode, no tool selection — **the object defines the verbs, not the tool.**
   *Consequence:* re-confirms invariant 9/13 and raises the bar: every
   surface gesture must be completable by finger, pencil, and keyboard
   command with identical results, and touch targets must never assume
   pencil precision (the cradle grip means the finger is often the effector
   even when a pencil is present).

5. **現在線 — the present is a thin, precise, always-on landmark.** The 5:35
   → 5:38 now-line is minute-exact, renders through everything, and the whole
   session is the user arranging objects AGAINST it. *Consequence:* the
   plugin has no "now" anywhere. Give the 台帳/triage/trail surfaces a
   現在地 line — the reading position / session boundary as a thin always-on
   rail the eye can arrange against, and make the one fixed back-stroke land
   AT it. Cheap, and it is the Calendar feeling more than any animation is.

6. **小さな確定 — micro-commits, never ceremonies.** Each grab-pull-release
   commits instantly and separately (top lip 4:45, release; bottom lip 6:00,
   release); undo is elsewhere and trusted; no confirmation exists in the
   whole eight minutes. *Consequence:* every carve/trim/ratify gesture in the
   inspector commits on release, one micro-action at a time, reversibility
   carried by the record (filled holes stay visible) rather than by dialogs.

7. **道具は手元に湧く — tools materialize at the point of work.** Squeeze →
   palette at the pen tip → pick → it dissolves on first ink; the same
   palette also exists folded as a parked puck (both park AND summon, they
   are complements, floating-rail already knows this). Lasso-then-act: the
   selection is held while the verb palette appears NEXT TO it.
   *Consequence:* selection-echo card = our squeeze palette — it must always
   materialize at the selection, die on next input, and resummon for free
   (command + hotkey since the OS keeps squeeze). The lasso lesson for
   TokenCanvas / ink: selection first, verbs arrive at the selection, never a
   trip to chrome.

8. **世界は持ち物の下を流れる — the world scrolls under a held thing.**
   Auto-scroll during carry; the day flips under the object; the panel, grid
   and now-line keep rendering; nothing pauses because the hand is full.
   *Consequence:* holding a specimen (HoldStore) must never lock navigation —
   suite-nav place-changes with a full hand are legal and cheap, and the held
   chip visibly persists across the crossing (it already survives view
   switches; the film adds: it must remain VISIBLE while the world moves,
   not just recoverable after).

9. **注意の層は膜を越えて随行する — the lookup layer rides along.** The
   dictionary strip pulled over Calendar is available over Notes two seconds
   later; the glance costs nothing and the destination app never knows.
   *Consequence:* inside our walls, the analog is the suite-nav stack plus
   trail chips — the last surface as a strip that follows you, entry-peek as
   a two-second glance (HoverPeek/echo card already merged), never a room.
   Outside our walls the five-app loop is permanent — so the tray mark with
   scene (the membrane law) stays the crossing contract; these reels show
   the user LIVING that loop at second-scale, which is the latency budget
   the plugin must meet: **a glance is ~2s; anything slower is a trip.**

## §3 合流 — Monokakido × Calendar, the reconciliation

The canon line stands: *Monokakido gives place without memory; Calendar gives
physics without depth.* The reels make the synthesis concrete:

> **The entry is a place AND a thing.** Monokakido contributes the place-half:
> fixed stations, paddles, sovereign selection, free symmetric return. Calendar
> contributes the thing-half: mintable, carriable, addressed, two-projection,
> micro-committed. The plugin's specimen — which alone has an INTERIOR (corpus,
> 分類, attestations, holes) — must be both: entered like a room, handled like
> a block.

Per-move consequences, in path order:

- **Move 1 (shipped):** hold-dock gains the 宛名札 — address chip on flick
  (§2.2) and lane-landing for repeated tosses (§2.3's coexistence; the tray
  already never drops — now it must also never *ask*).
- **Move 1½ 歩ける世界:** unchanged in shape, sharpened in spec: the back
  stroke lands at the 現在線 (§2.5); trail chips are the ride-along strip
  (§2.9); paddles/ghost per the Monokakido benchmark — **the 07-15 Monokakido
  ScreenRecording is still ungridded and still blocks this move** (standing
  debt, unchanged).
- **Move 2 inspector:** the film IS the spec — two projections, stored vs
  candidate registers, commit on release, editor floats while canvas stays
  live (§2.1, §2.6). Acceptance film: same 90 seconds as 1212 — popover open,
  hand works the object, panel follows, never a locked canvas.
- **Move 3 carve/rearrange/verdict:** duplicate-mint at the chip (§2.3) is
  the substrate for 並べ替える; swipe verdicts inherit micro-commit (§2.6);
  the lasso-then-act pattern (§2.7) is the carve's arming gesture on ink and
  on TokenCanvas alike.
- **Move 4 産出:** the compose buffer is a held thing over a moving world
  (§2.8) — drafting must not lock the corpus; grabs from 台帳 land with
  address chips.
- **膜 debts refreshed:** the ink experiment (Pencil-drag selected ink into
  ここにドロップ) rises in priority — reel 1214 shows ink is where language
  lands FIRST (〇引用… written by hand within seconds of the dictionary
  glance); the squeeze-palette latency (~400ms summon-to-pick) is the felt
  budget our echo card must match.

**Anti-copy clause** (the intuition is the import, not the widgets): do not
import the 15-minute snap grid (our axes are not time), the day-column
metaphor, or the points economy (+30ポイント is the user's own game — filed
as a hole: *what would a self-authored reward riding a block of plugin work
even be?* — machine offers, hand decides, nothing auto-fills it). And none of
this asks for animation polish — every law above is about WHERE information
appears and WHEN commits happen, which ships crude with knobs exposed.

## §4 契 — what would make this session count

hold/inspector ships a film script whose beats are §2's laws — (1) drag a mark
with the panel open and watch stored/candidate/commit; (2) flick a chip and
read its address mid-air; (3) long-press-mint a duplicate and see it lane
beside its sibling; (4) do all three with finger while the pencil stays in the
hand. One sentence spoken per wrongness; the streak is the metric. 1212–1214
join 1082/1144/1159 in the film ledger as the Calendar corpus — the "still
unanalysed Apple Calendar" the user flagged should be pulled into the same
grid when it surfaces; if it exists on Drive alongside the 07-15 Monokakido
